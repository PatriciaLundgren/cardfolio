Color

Lilac is the accent: it marks what you can tap. Everything else stays dark and quiet so your cards bring the color. The card colors from the logo are for illustration and card backgrounds, not interface controls.

Interface

Type

Instrument Sans, a crisp grotesque with a little warmth and quirk. Sentence case for every title, label and button.

Large titleInstrument Sans 600 · 32/1.1

Body text for everything you readInstrument Sans 400 · 16/1.45

Components

One primary action per screen, in lilac. Secondary actions sit on a dark surface. Corners are soft: 12 for controls, 16 for groups, 14 for cards.
